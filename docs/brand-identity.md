# Editorial identity

The updated identity pairs a rounded MK monogram with a restrained name wordmark. The M and K share a stem; the open strokes remain legible at small sizes. This is original vector artwork, with PNG exports for browser and home-screen icons.

- Primary wordmark: `public/images/brand/mk-wordmark-v2.svg`
- Standalone monogram: `public/images/brand/mk-symbol-v2.svg`
- Responsive, theme-aware site logo: `components/BrandLogo.jsx`
- Site-wide editorial styling: `app/brand.css`
- Browser and Apple icons: `app/icon.png`, `app/apple-icon.png`
- PWA icons: `public/images/logo-icon-192.png`, `public/images/logo-icon-512.png`, `public/images/logo-icon-512-maskable.png`

Colors: ivory `#faf8f4`, forest `#40513d`, charcoal `#252820`, muted olive `#65665e`. Headings use Georgia with a readable Arial body. The dark theme uses the same layout and reverses the monogram colors.

The shared styling covers the homepage, services and their detail pages, portfolio and case studies, articles and resource library, About, contact, checkout, resume and policy pages. Generated article covers use the same palette. Real client images and project evidence retain their original colors.
